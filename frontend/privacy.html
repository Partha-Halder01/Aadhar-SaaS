1

Introduction & Purpose

  • Instant Online Seva operates as an automated digital print formatting and PAN retrieval portal for CSC kiosks, cyber cafe retailers, and individual customers across India.
  • This policy describes exactly what data we collect, how it is processed, and how customer documents are strictly protected and deleted.
  • By using our portal, you acknowledge and agree to the simple data handling practices outlined below.
2

Information We Collect (Point to Point)

  • Account Details: Name, mobile phone number, email address, and password for creating and securing your merchant account.
  • Document Files: PDFs or image scans (e.g. e-Aadhaar, Voter slip, Ayushman card) uploaded exclusively to convert and format into printable CR-80 PVC sheets.
  • Demographic Query Inputs: Specific details (e.g. Aadhaar number, name, DOB) provided voluntarily for lost PAN card search services.
  • Wallet & Payment References: 12-digit UPI UTR reference numbers and payment timestamps for wallet recharge verification.
  • What We Never Collect: We never ask for or store your bank passwords, ATM PINs, UPI MPINs, or biometric fingerprint files.
3

How We Use Your Information (Point to Point)

  • Fulfilling Orders: To crop, align, and generate 300 DPI high-definition printable PVC cards requested by you.
  • Order Download & Delivery: To provide the completed print-ready PDF file in your account dashboard.
  • Wallet Ledger Accuracy: To credit wallet balance upon UTR verification and maintain your live transaction history.
  • Account Security: To verify login via OTP, prevent unauthorized access, and protect your funds.
  • No Unsolicited Marketing: We do not spam your phone number or email with third-party marketing calls or promotional messages.
4

Data Storage & Auto-Deletion (Point to Point)

  • 24 to 48-Hour Permanent Deletion: Uploaded source files and generated print PDFs are kept temporarily so you can download them. Once downloaded or within 24 to 48 hours, they are automatically and permanently purged from server disks.
  • Zero Permanent Document Archive: We do not keep any permanent archive or database of citizens' personal identity cards.
  • Encrypted Storage: Any file uploaded temporarily is held in protected server storage accessible only by your authenticated login session.
  • 256-Bit SSL Transport: Every upload and download request is protected by modern HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
Strict Auto-Purge Policy: Once an order is processed, please download your delivery PDF immediately. Our automated system permanently cleans old order files to ensure citizen privacy.
5

Third-Party Sharing (Point to Point)

  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any customer information or document scans to third parties or advertising brokers.
  • No External Trackers: We do not embed third-party advertising trackers or behavioral profiling tools on our service portals.
  • Legal Compliance Only: Information will only be disclosed if strictly mandated by applicable Indian laws, judicial court warrants, or authorized government directives.
6

Your Rights & Control (Point to Point)

  • View Your Data: Access your complete profile details, order history, and wallet ledger anytime directly inside your dashboard.
  • Update Profile: Edit your name, phone number, and password from the user settings page.
  • Request Immediate File Removal: If you wish to delete a file before the automated 24-hour cycle, you can raise an instant support ticket.
  • Account Closure: You can request full deactivation and deletion of your account at any time through our support team.
7

Contact & Grievance Support (Point to Point)

  • For any questions, data deletion requests, or privacy concerns, you can directly reach our support desk.
  • All grievances are reviewed and answered within 24 business hours.
