1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Instant Online Seva ("Portal", "we", "us", or "our"). We provide automated digital card formatting, 300 DPI CR-80 PVC sheet preparation, and lost document lookup assistance for digital retailers, CSC operators, cyber cafe owners, and individual citizens across India.

This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising dedication to safeguarding personal data in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

2. Information We Collect

To provide rapid automated processing, we collect only the strictly necessary information required to fulfill your requested services:

  • Retailer Account Details: Business name, operator name, active mobile number, and email address for authentication and transaction verification.
  • Document Processing Input: Files submitted strictly for printing (such as password-protected e-Aadhaar PDFs, Voter slips, or Ayushman health documents) and demographic identifiers (such as Aadhaar numbers or EPIC numbers) required for retrieval queries.
  • Wallet & Billing Information: 12-digit UPI UTR/transaction reference numbers and transaction timestamps. We do not store banking passwords or UPI PINs.

3. How We Use Document Data

All information collected is used exclusively for the explicit purpose for which it was provided by the retailer:

CR-80 PVC Vector Formatting
Automated cropping, dual-sided alignment, and high-DPI rasterization for printer tray output.
Legitimate Number Retrieval
Direct verification against official repository databases to assist citizens in recovering lost identification.

4. 256-Bit Security & Auto-Purging

Security is built directly into our infrastructure core:

Automated File Lifecycle Purging
Uploaded PDF source files and generated print outputs are strictly retained only for the duration necessary for the retailer to download the finished print (typically 24 to 48 hours), after which they are permanently deleted from our servers.

All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL). Database entries are shielded behind private firewalls with strict role-based access control.

5. Non-Disclosure & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal customer information or uploaded citizen documents to third parties or advertising networks.

Information is only disclosed if mandatory under Indian law, court order, or written directive from law enforcement authorities in the course of criminal investigations.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

As an authorized account holder, you have the right to:

  • Review and inspect your complete order ledger and wallet transaction logs at any time from your dashboard.
  • Request the immediate manual deletion of processed file copies from our storage.
  • Request closure and deletion of your retailer portal account by submitting a support ticket.
