Who we are

Online Digital Service is an online service business run by Mominul Islam from Murshidabad, West Bengal, India. Through onlinedigitalservice.xyz we help individuals, cyber cafés and small retailers get everyday document work done quickly, with clear prices and trackable orders.

What we offer

  • Card & document printing: PVC card prints and document prints prepared from documents you provide.
  • Citizen-service assistance: help with preparing and submitting applications for services such as PAN and Voter ID.
  • Document services: other document-related help listed on our services page.

The full list of services, with the price and expected delivery time for each, is shown on our services section and in your dashboard.

How it works

  1. Create a free account using your email and mobile number (verified by OTP).
  2. Add money to your wallet securely through Razorpay using UPI, cards, net banking or wallets.
  3. Choose a service, fill in the required details and upload documents. The price is deducted from your wallet.
  4. Track your order in the dashboard and download the deliverable when it's completed.

Our commitments

  • Transparent pricing — you see the price before you order.
  • Secure payments — all payments are processed by Razorpay; we never see your card or UPI details.
  • Privacy first — your documents are stored privately and used only to deliver your order. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Fair refunds — orders we can't complete are refunded. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
Independent service provider: Online Digital Service is a private business and is not affiliated with, or acting on behalf of, UIDAI, the Income Tax Department, NSDL/Protean, UTIITSL, the Election Commission of India or any other government authority.
