1

Core Refund Principle (Point to Point)

  • No Service, No Fee: If our platform cannot successfully generate your requested print file or retrieve the verified record, you are never charged.
  • Direct Wallet Credit: All services are billed via your internal prepaid wallet, and all refunds are credited instantly back to your portal wallet balance.
  • Zero Cancellation Penalty: No administrative fees, deductions, or penalty charges are ever withheld on failed orders.
2

When You Get a 100% Automatic Refund (Point to Point)

  • Record Not Found: Inquiries (like PAN Find) where the government registry returns no matching record for the provided Aadhaar number.
  • Upstream Server Timeout: When official verification portals experience temporary maintenance, server downtime, or gateway failure.
  • PDF Parsing Failure: If an uploaded PDF file is corrupted, password-protected with an incorrect key, or unreadable by our formatting engine.
  • Admin Rejection: When a manual card request is reviewed and rejected by the administrator due to illegible source scans.
  • Refund Speed: Automated wallet reversals occur within 1 to 5 seconds of the transaction failure and are logged in your wallet passbook.
Instant Automated Reversals: You don't need to open a ticket for failed orders. The portal engine detects the error and refunds your wallet balance immediately.
3

UPI QR Wallet Recharge Reconciliation (Point to Point)

  • Instant UTR Verification: Once you make payment to the portal's UPI QR code and submit the 12-digit bank UTR reference, funds are credited within 10–30 seconds.
  • Bank Debited but UTR Pending: In rare cases of bank network delays, the top-up settles automatically once the banking clearance ping confirms (typically within 15–30 minutes).
  • Failed Gateway Reversals: If your bank account is debited but the amount is not received by our merchant account, the banking network will auto-reverse the money back to your bank account within 2–5 business days per RBI rules.
  • Manual Support Escalation: If a valid recharge does not reflect within 30 minutes, submit a support ticket with the bank UTR and payment screenshot for instant manual clearance.
4

Non-Refundable Circumstances (Point to Point)

  • Successful Delivery: Any service where the PDF card print file or verified PAN number was successfully generated and delivered to your dashboard for download.
  • User Typo / Incorrect Input: If you input an incorrect Aadhaar number, wrong spelling, or wrong date of birth, but the query successfully executed and returned a valid result based on that input.
  • Cancellation After Generation: Order cancellation requests submitted after the automated engine has already generated the completed print file.
Input Accuracy Check: Please double-check 12-digit Aadhaar numbers, EPIC codes, and spelling before submitting to avoid charges on incorrect customer queries.
5

Defective Print Layouts & Free Reprints (Point to Point)

  • Free Regeneration: If an output PDF has graphical distortion, barcode truncation, or alignment defects caused by our formatting engine, we will regenerate it free of charge.
  • 48-Hour Claim Window: Submit a support ticket within 48 hours of order completion with your Order ID and a screenshot of the defect.
  • Zero Extra Cost: Our technical team will regenerate and re-deliver the corrected print file without debiting any additional wallet points.
6

Account Closure & Balance Withdrawal (Point to Point)

  • Withdrawal Right: Retailers wishing to close their account may withdraw their deposited, unused wallet balance.
  • Direct Bank Payout: Unused deposited funds (excluding promotional joining bonus credits) are transferred to the retailer's primary verified bank account via IMPS/NEFT.
  • Standard Timeline: Balance withdrawal requests are verified and settled within 5–7 banking business days.
