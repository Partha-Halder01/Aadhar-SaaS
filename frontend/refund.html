1. Foundational Refund Principle

At Instant Online Seva, we value the trust of our retail network. Our operational ethos is straightforward: if our platform cannot successfully fulfill the requested service, you will not be charged.

All financial transactions on our platform occur via the internal Retailer Wallet. Consequently, refunds are credited directly to your portal wallet balance for immediate reuse on subsequent orders.

2. Automated Wallet Reversals

Our backend engine automatically tracks the outcome of every request. You will receive an immediate automated 100% wallet refund in the following situations:

  • Data Not Found: Inquiries such as PAN Find where the government database returns no matching record for the provided Aadhaar number.
  • Server Timeout or Upstream Error: When official verification portals experience unexpected downtime or gateway timeouts during processing.
  • PDF Rendering Failure: If an uploaded PDF is password-locked, corrupted, or unsupported and the formatting engine cannot produce the output.
  • Manual Order Rejection: When a manual card request is reviewed by the portal administrator and rejected due to illegible source documents.
Timeline: Automated refunds occur instantly within 1 to 5 seconds of the transaction failure and are logged transparently in your Passbook / Wallet Statement.

3. QR Wallet Recharge Reconciliation

Retailers recharge portal wallets via dynamic UPI QR codes.

  • Successful UTR Verification: Once you submit the 12-digit bank UTR reference code, funds reflect in your wallet within 10–30 seconds.
  • Bank Debited, UTR Pending: In rare cases where your bank debits your account during UPI network degradation, the transaction will settle automatically once the NPCI/bank clearance ping is received (usually within 15–30 minutes).
  • Failed Banking Gateway: If funds are deducted by your bank but not received by our merchant collection account, the amount will be automatically returned to your original source bank account within 2–5 banking working days per RBI guidelines.

4. Non-Refundable Deliverables

Due to the digital nature of immediate PDF generation and consumable API fees, refunds cannot be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Successful Delivery: Any service where the PDF file or PAN number was successfully generated, verified, and delivered to your dashboard for download.
  • User Input Mistakes: If you input an incorrect Aadhaar number, wrong spelling, or erroneous demographic info, but the database returned a valid record matching that query.
  • Change of Mind: Cancellation requests submitted after the automated engine has already executed the generation process.

5. Defective Print Layout & Free Reprints

If the output generated by our system exhibits graphical distortion, barcode truncation, or alignment flaws attributable to our formatting engine:

You can request a Free System Re-run or Correction within 48 hours of order generation. Open a support ticket with your Order ID and our technical team will regenerate the corrected file without deducting additional points.

6. Account Closure & Balance Withdrawal

Retailers choosing to discontinue portal operations may apply for account closure. Unutilized, deposited wallet balances (excluding promotional joining bonus credits) can be refunded to the retailer's verified primary bank account via NEFT/IMPS:

  • A standard banking processing fee of 2.5% or ₹30 (whichever is higher) may be deducted for external bank settlement.
  • Settlement requests are reviewed and disbursed within 5–7 banking business days after KYC verification.
